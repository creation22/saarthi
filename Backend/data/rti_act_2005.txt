Right to Information Act, 2005 — excerpts for retrieval

Section 6 — Request for obtaining information
A person who desires to obtain any information under this Act shall make a request in writing or through electronic means in English or Hindi or in the official language of the area in which the application is being made, to the Central Public Information Officer or State Public Information Officer, as the case may be, of the public authority.

Section 7 — Disposal of request
The Central Public Information Officer or State Public Information Officer, as the case may be, shall dispose of a request for information within thirty days of the receipt of the request. If information concerns the life or liberty of a person, the same shall be provided within forty-eight hours.

Section 8 — Exemption from disclosure
Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act, there shall be no obligation to give any citizen information disclosure of which would prejudicially affect the sovereignty and integrity of India, security, strategic, scientific or economic interests of the State, relations with foreign State, or lead to incitement of an offence; or information which has been expressly forbidden to be published by any court or tribunal.

Section 18 — Powers and functions of Information Commissions
The Central Information Commission or State Information Commission, as the case may be, shall receive and inquire into a complaint from any person who has been unable to submit a request because of non-appointment of a Public Information Officer, refusal of access, failure to respond within time limits, or fees regarded as unreasonable.

Section 19 — Appeal
Any person who does not receive a decision within the time specified or is aggrieved by a decision may prefer an appeal to such officer who is senior in rank to the Public Information Officer within thirty days from the expiry of such period or from the receipt of such a decision.
