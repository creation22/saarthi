Consumer Protection Act, 2019 — excerpts for retrieval

Section 2(7) — Definition of consumer
Consumer means any person who buys any goods or avails any service for a consideration but does not include a person who obtains goods for resale or for any commercial purpose, or avails any service for any commercial purpose.

Section 35 — Jurisdiction of District Commission
Subject to the other provisions of this Act, the District Commission shall have jurisdiction to entertain complaints where the value of the goods or services paid as consideration does not exceed one crore rupees.

Section 38 — Manner in which complaint shall be made
A complaint shall be filed within two years from the date on which the cause of action has arisen, in such form and manner as may be prescribed, before the District Commission within the local limits of whose jurisdiction the opposite party resides or carries on business or works for gain.

Section 39 — Relief available
If the District Commission is satisfied that any goods are defective or there is deficiency in service, it may order removal of defect, replacement, refund, compensation for loss or injury, discontinuation of unfair trade practice, withdrawal of hazardous goods, or payment of adequate costs.

Section 47 — Appeal
Any person aggrieved by an order made by the District Commission may prefer an appeal to the State Commission within forty-five days from the date of order.
